How to play

Flap: tap the screen, click, or press Space.

Score: fly through the gap in each pair of pipes to earn a point.

Crash: touching a pipe or the ground ends the run.

Pause: use the pause button, or press P or Esc.

Medals: bronze at 10, silver at 20, gold at 30, platinum at 40.
